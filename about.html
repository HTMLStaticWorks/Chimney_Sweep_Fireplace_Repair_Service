FAQ

Venting Safety & Care Guidance

Find answers to the most common chimney maintenance questions to keep your home safe and warm.

The National Fire Protection Association (NFPA) Standard 211 states that chimneys, fireplaces, and vents should be inspected at least once a year. If you use your wood-burning fireplace frequently, a sweep is typically recommended for every cord of wood burned, or when soot accumulation reaches 1/8 inch.

Creosote is a highly flammable, dark, tar-like byproduct of burning wood that accumulates inside the chimney flue liner. If creosote build-up is neglected, it can easily ignite from a spark, causing a dangerous chimney fire that can crack the flue liner and spread to the rest of the home's structure.

A Level 1 inspection is a standard visual check of all readily accessible portions of the fireplace and chimney. A Level 2 inspection is more comprehensive, utilizing specialized chimney video cameras to examine the internal flue joints, liners, and clearances that are not visible to the naked eye. It is required during real estate transactions or after a chimney fire or weather event.
