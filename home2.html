OUR CORE PRINCIPLES

A Signature Standard of Safety

Venting heating systems inside a luxury home requires a combination of structural engineering, active safety credentials, and neat execution methods.

01

Venting Precision

We calibrate draft controls and verify clear airflow routes to ensure your wood or gas systems draw air efficiently and burn cleaner.

02

Structural Preservation

Preserve your hearth's timeless beauty. We repair historical clay liners, reline flues with premium stainless steel, and secure weathered masonry crowns.

03

Preventive Wellness

Avoid off-season decay. Our thorough, proactive checkups detect freeze-thaw cracking and nesting blockages before they become costly hazards.

DIAGNOSTIC EXCELLENCE

Advanced Video Diagnostics & Integrity Tests

We utilize specialized, self-leveling chimney cameras to explore every inch of your flue. This non-destructive diagnostic process exposes hidden defects, flue obstruction hazards, and lining degradation with crystal clarity.

High-Resolution Internal Mapping

Examine internal flue liners, joint segments, and firebox interfaces in real-time, capturing detailed visual records.

Comprehensive Hearth Analysis

Evaluate clearances to combustibles, lintel placement, and damper seals against modern building safety standards.

Our Technical Evaluation Protocol

  • Verification of flue tile alignment and joint integrity.
  • Creosote and combustible residue density testing.
  • Outer masonry crown and flashing waterproofing checks.
  • Damper linkage operation and sealing performance.
  • Combustion air inlet and draft stability evaluations.
