01 / SAFETY EVALUATION

Level 2 Video Chimney Inspection

My standard evaluation goes far beyond checking with a flashlight. I run a specialized dual-angle camera system up the entire length of your flue, capturing real-time high-definition footage of every mortar joint, clay tile, and metal connection.

Why a Video Flue Scan is Crucial:

  • Exposes Hidden Gaps: Even a 1/16-inch crack in a clay flue tile can allow toxic carbon monoxide to seep into your home's walls.
  • Identifies Flue Obstructions: Pinpoints blockages, animal nests, or collapsed chimney masonry.
  • Real Estate Compliance: Meets NFPA 211 standards required during home transfers.

My Inspection Process Timeline:

Step 1

Hearth Area Prep & Protection

I seal off the fireplace opening with double-layer sheeting and position a high-power HEPA extractor to prevent any dust intrusion into your living space.

Step 2

Internal Video Scanning

I guide the camera through the throat, smoke chamber, and flue to the top of the crown, recording footage to detect structural shifts or fractures.

Step 3

External Evaluation

I scale the roof to inspect the chimney cap, brick flashings, concrete crown, and brick mortar exterior stability.

Step 4

Comprehensive Digital Safety Report

I provide you with a written report containing high-resolution snapshots of your flue interiors, indicating pass/fail status for each safety point.

02 / FLUE CLEANING

Dust-Free Chimney Sweeping

Standard sweep methods with simple wire brushes can scrape flue tiles or miss hard, glazed creosote deposits. I use advanced mechanical rotary whips combined with high-airflow containment to strip away dangerous glaze safely.

Creosote Danger

Creosote is a highly flammable, sticky byproduct of burning wood. When it builds up, a single spark from your fireplace can ignite a chimney fire exceeding 2,000°F, threatening your entire home structure.

My Clean Pledge

My HEPA extraction units create constant negative pressure. I guarantee that no soot, smoke smell, or debris will contaminate your furniture, rugs, or air quality.

03 / STRUCTURAL MASONRY

Fireplace & Flue Masonry Restoration

Freezing temperatures and rain cause brick chimney stacks to crack and decay. I apply traditional tuckpointing methods, replace cracked firebox bricks, install stainless steel flue liners, and repair concrete crowns to seal moisture out.

My expertise covers fireplace damper replacements, structural parging of the smoke chamber to improve drafts, and custom brick veneer restorations to preserve the heritage style of your home.
